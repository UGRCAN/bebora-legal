English

Bebora helps parents and legal guardians organize baby records. It is not a medical diagnosis, treatment or emergency service. Consult a healthcare professional for health decisions.

Core free features can be used as a guest. Account-enabled versions offer optional Google or Apple sign-in. Record only information you are authorized to access and protect your sign-in account. Signing in does not back up baby records to the cloud; create a backup before changing devices.
